## Headline results {page=3}

Audited figures for the financial year ended 31 December 2025:

| Measure | FY2025 | FY2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | **GBP 2.8 billion** | GBP 2.1 billion |
| Gross margin | 61.4% | 58.9% |
| Operating profit | GBP 412 million | GBP 246 million |
| Closing headcount | 11,240 | 9,870 |

Revenue for FY2025 was **GBP 2.8 billion**, representing growth of 33 percent on the
prior year. These are the audited figures and supersede all pre-close estimates.

## Note on reported figures {page=4}

The FY2025 investor press release (MAT-NW-PR-2025) quotes a bookings figure of GBP 2.9
billion. Bookings and revenue are different measures: bookings include contracted value not
yet recognised. The audited revenue figure is GBP 2.8 billion and is the correct figure for
any statement about revenue.

Any statement of FY2025 revenue materially above the audited figure recorded above is not
supported by these accounts, whatever currency it is expressed in.

## Segment detail {page=5}

Platform subscriptions contributed GBP 1.9 billion, professional services GBP 0.6 billion,
and hardware resale GBP 0.3 billion.
