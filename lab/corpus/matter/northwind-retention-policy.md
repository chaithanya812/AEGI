## Retention periods {page=2}

| Record class | Retention | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Executed contracts | 7 years after expiry | limitation period |
| Financial records | 7 years | statutory |
| Employee records | 6 years after leaving | statutory |
| System access logs | 18 months | security policy |
| AI interaction traces and verdicts | **7 years** | audit and regulatory defence |

The 18-month figure applies to system access logs only. The retention period for AI
interaction traces is seven years, revised upward from the 3-year period in the superseded
2021 policy (MAT-NW-POL-RET-2021).

## AI system records {page=3}

Where an automated system produces an assessment relied upon in a decision, the record must
retain: the input, the output, the evidence consulted, the verdict, the identity of the
decision rule applied, and a tamper-evident signature over the assessment.

Records must be reproducible on request within five working days.
