## Clause 14 — Limitation of liability {page=9}

14.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death, personal injury or fraud.

14.2 Save as set out in clause 14.3, each party's aggregate liability is limited to 125% of
the Charges paid in the preceding twelve months.

14.3 **The indemnity cap at clause 16 is limited to USD 10,000,000 in aggregate over the
life of the Agreement, and expressly survives termination.** For the avoidance of doubt the
survival period is unlimited in time.

## Clause 27 — Survival {page=15}

27.2 Clauses 13 (Confidentiality), 14 (Limitation of liability), 16 (Indemnity), 21
(Data protection) and 29 (Governing law) survive termination or expiry.

**Clause 14 is expressly listed and therefore survives.** A claim that the Acme indemnity
cap does not survive termination is contradicted by clauses 14.3 and 27.2 read together.

## Clause 16 — Indemnity {page=10}

16.1 Each party indemnifies the other against third-party intellectual property claims
arising from materials it supplies.
