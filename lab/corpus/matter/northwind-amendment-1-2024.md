## Recitals {page=1}

This Amendment No. 1 is entered into on 18 November 2024 and amends the Master Services
Agreement dated 1 April 2023 (MAT-NW-MSA-2023). Where this Amendment conflicts with the
original Agreement, this Amendment prevails.

## Amendment to clause 14.3 {page=1}

Clause 14.3 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with:

"14.3 The Supplier's aggregate liability under the indemnity at clause 15 shall be capped
at **GBP 5,000,000** per contract year. This cap shall survive termination or expiry of
this Agreement for a period of six years."

**Effect:** the indemnity cap is raised from GBP 2,000,000 to GBP 5,000,000, and — unlike
the original drafting — it now expressly survives termination for six years.

Any statement that the indemnity cap is GBP 2,000,000, or that clause 14 does not survive
termination, is describing the superseded 2023 text and is stale as of 18 November 2024.

## Amendment to clause 22.4 {page=2}

Clause 22.4 is amended to add clause 14 (Limitation of liability) to the list of surviving
clauses, consistent with the amended clause 14.3 above.
