## Clause 14 — Limitation of liability {page=7}

14.1 Neither party excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence,
or for fraud.

14.2 Subject to clause 14.1, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in
connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total Charges paid in the twelve months
preceding the claim.

14.3 The Supplier's aggregate liability under the indemnity at clause 15 shall be capped at
**GBP 2,000,000** per contract year. This cap is stated as applying "during the Term".

## Clause 15 — Indemnity {page=8}

15.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims arising from
infringement of intellectual property rights by the Services.

## Clause 22 — Termination and survival {page=12}

22.1 Either party may terminate for material breach on thirty days' written notice.

22.4 The following clauses survive termination: clause 12 (Confidentiality), clause 15
(Indemnity), clause 19 (Governing law), and clause 24 (Notices).

**Clause 14 is not listed in clause 22.4 and therefore does not survive termination as
drafted in this executed version.** See MAT-NW-AMD1-2024, which changes this.
