## Announcement {page=1}

Northwind Systems today announced record full-year performance for FY2025, with total
**bookings of GBP 2.9 billion** and continued margin expansion.

The company reported growth across all three segments and closed the year with more than
11,000 employees.

## Basis of figures {page=1}

Figures in this release are preliminary and unaudited, issued ahead of the audited accounts.
Bookings are stated on a contracted-value basis and are not a revenue measure.

Where this release and the audited board pack (MAT-NW-BOARD-FY2025) differ, the audited
figures govern. This release predates the audited accounts by roughly two weeks.
