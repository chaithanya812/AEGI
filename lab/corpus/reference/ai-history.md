## Foundational papers {page=1}

The term "artificial intelligence" was coined for the Dartmouth Summer Research Project of
1956. The perceptron was described by Frank Rosenblatt in 1958.

Backpropagation was popularised for neural networks by Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams in a
1986 Nature paper. AlexNet won the ImageNet competition in 2012, and is generally taken as
the start of the modern deep learning era.

The transformer architecture was introduced in "Attention Is All You Need" by Vaswani et
al. at NeurIPS in 2017. BERT followed in 2018.

## Benchmarks and claims {page=1}

Reported accuracy figures are meaningful only against a named dataset, split and metric. A
bare percentage with no dataset attached is not a verifiable claim.

## Hallucination {page=2}

In the language-model literature, "hallucination" refers to fluent output that is not
grounded in the model's inputs or in fact. Surveys consistently find that expressed
confidence in model output correlates poorly with factual accuracy, which is why
confidence stated in prose cannot be used as evidence of correctness.
