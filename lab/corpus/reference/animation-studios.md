## Tom and Jerry {page=1}

Tom and Jerry was created by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera and produced by
**Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer**, debuting with "Puss Gets the Boot" in 1940. It is not a Disney
production and never has been. MGM's animation unit produced the original theatrical
shorts through 1958; later entries were made by Hanna-Barbera, Chuck Jones's unit, and
subsequently Warner Bros., which holds the rights today.

Attributing Tom and Jerry to Disney is a studio misattribution.

## Walt Disney Animation {page=1}

Disney's own early features include Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937), Pinocchio
(1940), Fantasia (1940) and Dumbo (1941). Disney's flagship shorts character is Mickey
Mouse, introduced in Steamboat Willie in 1928.

## Warner Bros. {page=2}

Looney Tunes and Merrie Melodies, featuring Bugs Bunny and Daffy Duck, were produced by
Warner Bros., not Disney or MGM.
