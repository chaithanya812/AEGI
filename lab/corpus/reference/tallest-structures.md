## World records {page=1}

The Burj Khalifa in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, is the tallest building and the tallest
free-standing structure in the world at 828 metres, completed in 2010. It is emphatically
not the shortest tower by any measure; among notable towers worldwide it ranks first by
height.

The Merdeka 118 tower in Kuala Lumpur is the second tallest building at 679 metres.

## European records {page=1}

The tallest structure in Europe is the Lakhta Center in Saint Petersburg, Russia, at 462
metres, completed in 2019. The tallest structure in the European Union is the Varso Tower
in Warsaw, Poland, at 310 metres including its spire.

No structure in Europe exceeds 500 metres. Any claim that a European structure stands at
over 500 metres is inconsistent with the current record.

## The Eiffel Tower in context {page=2}

The Eiffel Tower, at 330 metres including aerials, is materially shorter than the Lakhta
Center and roughly 40 percent of the height of the Burj Khalifa. It held the world record
between 1889 and 1930 only.
