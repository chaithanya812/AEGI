## First flights {page=1}

Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space aboard Vostok 1 on 12 April 1961. Alan
Shepard followed on 5 May 1961. Valentina Tereshkova became the first woman in space aboard
Vostok 6 in June 1963.

## Moon landings {page=1}

Apollo 11 landed on the Moon on 20 July 1969. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the
surface while Michael Collins remained in lunar orbit. Six Apollo missions landed
successfully — Apollo 11, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 17 — placing twelve people on the surface in
total. Apollo 13 suffered an oxygen tank failure and returned without landing.

The last crewed lunar landing was Apollo 17 in December 1972. No crewed landing has taken
place since.

## Mars {page=2}

No human has travelled to Mars. All Mars missions to date have been robotic. Any statement
describing a completed crewed Mars landing describes an event that has not occurred.
