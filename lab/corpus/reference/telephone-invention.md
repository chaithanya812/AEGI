## The 1876 patent {page=1}

Alexander Graham Bell was granted US Patent 174,465 for the telephone on 7 March 1876,
having filed on 14 February 1876. Bell demonstrated speech transmission to his assistant
Thomas Watson on 10 March 1876.

Bell founded the Bell Telephone Company in 1877.

## Competing claims {page=1}

Elisha Gray filed a caveat describing a similar liquid transmitter on the same day Bell
filed his application, producing one of the most litigated priority disputes in patent
history.

Antonio Meucci demonstrated a voice-transmitting device in the 1850s and filed a caveat in
1871 that he did not renew. In 2002 the US House of Representatives passed a resolution
recognising Meucci's contribution. Johann Philipp Reis built an earlier device that
transmitted tone but not intelligible speech.

The attribution of the telephone to Bell is therefore correct as a matter of the 1876
patent record, while the broader question of first invention remains contested.
