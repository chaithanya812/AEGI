## Mountains {page=1}

Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level at 8,849 metres, on the border of
Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region of China. The 8,849-metre figure comes from the joint
Nepal–China survey published in December 2020, revising the earlier 8,848-metre value.

K2 is the second highest at 8,611 metres. Mauna Kea is taller measured from its base on the
sea floor, but not above sea level.

## Rivers and deserts {page=1}

The Nile and the Amazon are the two longest rivers, and which ranks first depends on where
the Amazon's source is taken to be; the Nile is conventionally cited at about 6,650 km. The
Amazon carries by far the greatest volume of water.

The Sahara is the largest hot desert at about 9.2 million square kilometres. Antarctica is
the largest desert of any kind.

## Oceans {page=2}

The Pacific is the largest and deepest ocean. Its deepest point, Challenger Deep in the
Mariana Trench, is approximately 10,935 metres below sea level.
